The player

Built to open the batting, and to turn a game with the ball.

A right-handed top-order batsman for MCC Green Academy in Muzaffargarh, with more than 1,000 runs from 100-plus matches — averaging 30.8 at a strike rate of 120, and a highest score of 134 not out. Right-arm off-break in support. He has represented his district at both PCB district and regional level, and captained the district's Under-19 side.

Recent scorecards show the range: 80 off 63 balls (10 fours, 5 sixes) against I.C.C. Multan, and 74 not out against R.C.C. — both for MCC Green. He has picked up multiple Man of the Match awards along the way, most recently at the Azadi Cup Under-17 Series in Muzaffargarh.
